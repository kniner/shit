Reading the workouts ›

Every move has an ⓘ button in the menu — tap it for step-by-step how-to. The shorthand:

3×10Sets × reps. Do 10 repetitions, rest, and repeat until you've done 3 sets total.
/ leg · / armThat rep count is per side — e.g. “3×10 / arm” is 10 reps on each arm.
roundA timed block of work, from boxing — usually 2–3 min of effort then ~1 min rest. “6×2 min rounds” = six 2-minute rounds.
finisherA short burst at the end of a workout to top it off.
zone 2Easy cardio you could hold a conversation through — the fat-burning, low-stress pace.
hip hingeBending by pushing your hips back with a flat back (not squatting down) — how deadlifts work.
RDLRomanian deadlift — a hip hinge that trains hamstrings and glutes.
DBDumbbell.
Adding weight (progressive overload) ›

Muscle grows when you gradually ask it to do more — that's progressive overload. On each lift, tap Log weight to record what you used and whether you hit all your sets and reps.

The rule the app follows:

Hit all repsNice — it suggests going up next time (≈5 lb on upper-body moves, ≈10 lb on squats & deadlifts).
Missed repsStay at the same weight and aim to complete every rep before adding load.

Log the weight you'd use for dumbbell moves consistently (e.g. always “per dumbbell”) so the trend stays honest. Small, steady jumps beat big leaps.

Why lift at all on a GLP-1 ›

On Zepbound a chunk of lost weight can be muscle, not just fat — and losing muscle slows your metabolism and makes regain easier. Lifting 3–4×/week plus enough protein is what tells your body to keep the muscle while the fat comes off. That's the whole point of the menu.

Don't train the same muscle group hard two days running — it rebuilds in the ~48h between sessions, not during them. That's what the back-to-back warning watches for.

Over a week, aim to hit legs, push and pull a similar number of times — lopsided training builds imbalances and nagging aches. The muscle-balance readout tracks your completed lifts over a rolling 7 days (by date, not calendar week) and flags whichever group is falling behind.
